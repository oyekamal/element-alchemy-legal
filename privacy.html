Privacy policy

Element Alchemy (Android app com.oyekamal.elementalchemy). Last updated 6 November 2026.

Short version: Element Alchemy does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

What the app stores

  • Your progress and settings (discovered substances and reactions, rank, quiz scores, your daily streak, rewards and score, the calendar days you played so the streak can be counted, sound and display settings, and whether you have seen the intro tour and tips) are saved in the app's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • A local error log of up to the last 50 technical errors (error message, a short code location, screen size, app version and time). It stays on your device. It is only shared if you choose Settings > About > Copy error report and paste it somewhere yourself. It contains no name, contact details, identifiers or game content you typed.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing from the game itself. The game works fully offline.
  • Update check (Android, when online). The app asks the Google Play Store app on your phone whether a newer version exists, using Google's in-app update library. This request is handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy; we receive nothing from it.
  • Links you tap. Source links in the Codex (for example to openstax.org) open in your browser only when you tap them.

Ads, purchases and third parties

There are no ads and no in-app purchases. The Android app contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Crash statistics that Android and Google Play collect for all apps (Play Console "Android vitals") are anonymous and aggregated by Google; we only see totals.

Backups

If Android backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include the game's saved progress in your personal Google backup so it comes back when you reinstall. That backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the Google Play update check and links you tap.
  • Vibrate: short haptic feedback. Turn it off in Settings > Haptics.

Children

Element Alchemy is made for teens (13+) and adults and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

Use Settings > Reset progress, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. That removes everything; there is no copy anywhere else.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or anything that sends data, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the change will be described here.
